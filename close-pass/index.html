A rule that changes the outcome

IF

predicted clearance < 0.80 m

THEN

wait behind the leading rider.

ELSE

overtake while maintaining clearance.

With the rule off, the rider attempts the pass regardless. If actual handlebar clearance reaches zero during overlap, the leading rider falls.

−

What proximity risks

Handlebar contact can disrupt balance. A startled rider can swerve. Less lateral space leaves less room for either person to correct their line.

+

What passing enables

Riders can travel at different speeds and continue their journey. This benefit comes from completing a pass with room to spare; reducing clearance adds no inherent safety benefit.
