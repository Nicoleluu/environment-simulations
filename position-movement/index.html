Nicole Lu / Position + MovementA spatial computing experiment

Aster Academy / Field notes

A hall made of relationships

Each landing is a location. Each aligned staircase is an edge in a changing graph. Rotate a flight and its upper landing changes; a route may appear or disappear.

Position becomes behavior

Adjacent: two landings share one aligned flight, with no intervening landing. An open route illuminates in gold. Above: the traveler is higher than the entrance. Between: the traveler is strictly inside their journey along its polyline, rather than geometrically collinear with its endpoints.

An embedded capability

The student carries a lantern spell. Within 1.2 units of the selected destination, it awakens the destination’s portal. The portal stays lit until a new journey or reset.

Movement has memory

Toward: Euclidean distance to the destination decreases between samples. Ascending: elevation increases. Past: total traveled length crosses the journey’s halfway mark; the event remains recorded until the next journey. A turn may briefly move away from the destination.

Travel uses the shortest available graph route. Flights cannot rotate while occupied. Decorative galleries and arches are scenery, not hidden routes. Coordinates are schematic units. This is an original fantasy academy inspired by shifting-stair architecture.
