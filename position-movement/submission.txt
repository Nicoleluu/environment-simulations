An original magical academy where rotating staircases change the relationships between seven fixed landings. Three position conditions—adjacent, above, and between along a path—are paired with three movement conditions: toward, ascending, and past the journey midpoint.

An aligned stair configuration reveals a traversable route. A student carries an intrinsic lantern spell that awakens the selected destination's portal on approach. The six-slide deck includes diagrams and recorded animations of stair rotation, ascent, and arrival.

Demo: choose Astral archive for direct adjacency; travel and return. Shift the stairs to disconnect the observatory. Choose Winter garden, align the route, and watch the lantern wake its portal. Individual flights can be clicked to rotate, or operated through Field notes. Occupied flights cannot rotate.

Built with AI assistance. Coordinates are schematic; decorative galleries are not traversable graph edges.
